CORE CURRICULUM

The 12 Essential Lean Manufacturing Modules

Master the standard operating principles and implementation toolkits driving global manufacturing excellence.

MODULE 01 • PRODUCTIVITY

OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)

Course Duration: 3 Days

The gold standard for measuring shop-floor manufacturing productivity and equipment health.

  • Measures performance across Availability, Performance, and Quality.
  • Identifies hidden losses due to equipment downtime, speed loss, or defects.
  • Helps maximize equipment utilization, line capacity, and plant output.
  • Drives continuous data-driven improvement in manufacturing efficiency.
Book OEE Lab Demo →
MODULE 02 • QUALITY

7 QC Tools (Quality Control)

Course Duration: 3 Days

Universal data-driven statistical tools for systematic problem solving and defect elimination.

  • Core tools for structured problem solving and root cause quality control.
  • Covers Pareto Chart, Fishbone (Ishikawa), Histogram, Control Chart, Scatter Diagram, Check Sheet & Flowchart.
  • Enables data-driven decision making and statistical root cause identification.
  • Forms the essential foundation of Six Sigma DMAIC and TQM practices.
Enroll in 7 QC Tools →
MODULE 03 • WORKFLOW

Kanban (Visual Scheduling)

Course Duration: 3 Days

Visual pull-based workflow management system to eliminate shop-floor inventory bottlenecks.

  • Visual scheduling system for agile shop-floor workflow management.
  • Drastically reduces overproduction and improves process transparency.
  • Promotes pull-based Just-In-Time (JIT) production and inventory control.
  • Enhances cross-functional team collaboration, accountability, and responsiveness.
Explore Kanban Workshop →
MODULE 04 • ZERO DEFECT

Poka-Yoke (Mistake Proofing)

Course Duration: 3 Days

Ingenious Japanese engineering mechanisms to guarantee 100% defect-free production.

  • Renowned Japanese engineering concept for foolproof error prevention.
  • Employs simple mechanical jigs, optical sensors, and electrical interlocks to avoid human mistakes.
  • Ensures zero-defect manufacturing, scrap reduction, and safety compliance.
  • Builds bulletproof reliability directly into assembly processes and products.
Hands-On Poka-Yoke →
MODULE 05 • EFFICIENCY

Single Piece Flow

Course Duration: 3 Days

Continuous flow manufacturing to eliminate Work-In-Progress (WIP) and compress lead time.

  • Moves one unit at a time seamlessly through each progressive assembly step.
  • Minimizes waiting time, batch accumulation, work-in-progress, and lead time.
  • Improves production flexibility and rapid responsiveness to customer demand.
  • Core foundational principle of Lean and Just-In-Time (JIT) production.
Learn Flow Optimization →
MODULE 06 • WASTE ELIMINATION

Lean Waste (TIMWOODS)

Course Duration: 3 Days

Structured methodology to hunt and eradicate the 8 deadly wastes in any operational process.

  • Identifies eight wastes: Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Waiting, Overproduction, Overprocessing, Defects, Skills.
  • Eliminates all non-value-adding activities across factory and office workflows.
  • Improves operational flow, resource efficiency, and direct bottom-line cost savings.
  • Encourages a company-wide culture of daily continuous improvement (Kaizen).
TIMWOODS Audit Training →
MODULE 07 • REAL-TIME ALERT

Andon (Visual Signal System)

Course Duration: 3 Days

Real-time visual problem reporting to halt defect propagation and escalate line issues.

  • Visual audio-light signaling system for real-time shop-floor problem reporting.
  • Alerts engineering and maintenance teams to production line issues instantly.
  • Promotes rapid response, root-cause resolution, and line-level accountability.
  • Strengthens communication and transparency between operators and supervisors.
Andon Simulation Lab →
MODULE 08 • TIME STUDY

Cycle Time & Takt Time

Course Duration: 3 Days

Time study and pacing analytics to balance line speeds with client demand rates.

  • Measures actual time taken to complete one full production or assembly cycle.
  • Helps identify station bottlenecks, line starvation, and micro-inefficiencies.
  • Enables accurate capacity planning, workstation balancing, and scheduling.
  • Key operational metric for Lean flow optimization against Takt Time.
Time Study Workshop →
MODULE 09 • THROUGHPUT

Bottleneck Analysis

Course Duration: 3 Days

Theory of Constraints (TOC) to identify and debottleneck capacity-limiting operations.

  • Identifies process steps and constraint stations that limit total throughput.
  • Focuses engineering improvement and debottlenecking efforts where they matter most.
  • Reduces production delays, inventory build-ups, and increases overall output.
  • Supports balanced, synchronized workflow and optimal resource utilization.
Bottleneck Diagnostics →
MODULE 10 • KAIZEN LOOP

PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)

Course Duration: 3 Days

The Deming scientific cycle for perpetual, disciplined organizational learning and growth.

  • Structured scientific approach for continuous, repeatable process improvement.
  • Encourages hypothesis experimentation, pilot testing, and learning from results.
  • Builds closed-loop feedback mechanisms for permanent standard optimization.
  • Universal foundation for Kaizen events, Six Sigma, and ISO quality systems.
PDCA Kaizen Workshop →
MODULE 11 • WORKPLACE 5S

5S Workplace Organization

Course Duration: 3 Days

Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain for an immaculate, visual workspace.

  • Sort (Seiri), Set in Order (Seiton), Shine (Seiso), Standardize (Seiketsu), Sustain (Shitsuke).
  • Creates an organized, clean, visual, and highly efficient shop-floor workspace.
  • Significantly improves operator safety, reduces search time, and boosts morale.
  • Forms the essential cultural foundation for Lean discipline and audit readiness.
5S Audit Masterclass →
MODULE 12 • VALUE STREAM

Value Stream Mapping (VSM)

Course Duration: 3 Days

End-to-end strategic visual mapping of information and material flow across operations.

  • Visual mapping tool to trace all material and information flows from supplier to client.
  • Identifies process waste, non-value-added waiting, and transformation opportunities.
  • Enables end-to-end operational optimization and future-state design.
  • Supports strategic executive planning for enterprise Lean digital transformation.
